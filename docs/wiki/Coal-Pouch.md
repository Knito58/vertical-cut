# Coal Pouch

Any bundle (modded bundles too) in a module slot, **without an amethyst shard** in it.

![The module screen with a coal pouch and a depth gauge: stored fuel and the pouch's worth below the slots](images/module-screen.png)

The orange bar on the right edge of the picture is the fuel gauge (see the [Depth Gauge](Depth-Gauge.md) picture).

## What it does
- The CRT then eats **only from the bundle**, never from your inventory.
- It eats **anything that burns in a furnace**, worth what it would burn there compared to coal:
  a coal block is 10 coals, a lava bucket 12.5 (you get the empty bucket back), sticks and
  saplings little. Small fuels are eaten in batches of about one coal's worth.
- The CRT can store up to 256 coals' worth. Whatever doesn't fit stays in the bundle.
- A thin bar beside the picture shows stored fuel plus the bundle (full at 64 coals). The
  tooltip shows both numbers.
- While the pouch has fuel, the picture never dies.

## Careful
It burns everything that burns, **enchanted wooden tools too**. Don't keep them in the pouch.

Put an amethyst shard into the pouch and it becomes a [Finder Bundle](Finder-Bundle.md) instead; then it
stops feeding the CRT.
