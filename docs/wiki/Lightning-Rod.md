# Lightning Rod

Any lightning rod in a module slot.

## What it does
It shields against **three quarters of the interference**.

Interference comes from:
- lit portals (nether portal, end portal, end gateway, not their frames) within about 6 blocks,
- the nearest enderman within 8 blocks (an angry one disturbs twice as much).

The closer the source, the more snow and buzzing in the picture.

The [Netherite CRT](Netherite-CRT.md) needs no lightning rod; nothing gets through to it.
