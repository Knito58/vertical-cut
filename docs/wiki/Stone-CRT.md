# Stone CRT

The starter device. A crude cave finder.

## Recipe
```
C G C      C = cobblestone, blackstone or cobbled deepslate
C T C      G = glass pane
C C C      T = torch
```
The recipe unlocks when you pick up cobblestone.

## What it shows
- 32×32 blocks, black and white on a black background.
- Caves (black), water (mid grey), lava (blinking).
- **No ores.**
- You as a stick figure.

## Its faults
- The picture **lags** behind when you walk.
- It **flickers** and buzzes now and then.
- No module slots.

## Fuel
One coal or charcoal per 4 minutes of scanning (adjustable), taken from your inventory when it
runs dry. Only burns while the cut is shown. The durability bar shows the coal that is burning now.

## Upgrade
Smithing table: torch + Stone CRT + [Copper Picture Tube](Picture-Tubes.md) → [Copper CRT](Copper-CRT.md).
