# Picture Tubes

The parts that upgrade a CRT on the smithing table, always with a **torch as the template**.
There are four. The Netherite CRT needs none; it uses the vanilla netherite upgrade.

| Tube | Recipe | Turns |
|---|---|---|
| Copper Picture Tube | 8 copper ingots around a torch | [Stone CRT](Stone-CRT.md) → [Copper CRT](Copper-CRT.md) |
| Iron Picture Tube | 8 iron ingots around a redstone torch | Copper CRT → [Iron CRT](Iron-CRT.md) |
| Gold Picture Tube | 8 gold ingots around a redstone torch | Iron CRT → [Gold CRT](Gold-CRT.md) |
| Diamond Picture Tube | 8 diamonds around a redstone torch | Gold CRT → [Diamond CRT](Diamond-CRT.md) |

```
X X X      X = copper ingot, iron ingot, gold ingot or diamond
X T X      T = torch (copper) or redstone torch (all others)
X X X
```

The recipe for each tube appears in the recipe book when you pick up the material or the CRT to
be upgraded. Fuel and modules carry over to the new CRT.
