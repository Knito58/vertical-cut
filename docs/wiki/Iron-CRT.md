# Iron CRT

The big jump: color.

> *Grey shows the bones. Iron shows the blood.*

## How to make it
Smithing table: torch + [Copper CRT](Copper-CRT.md) + [Iron Picture Tube](Picture-Tubes.md).

## What it shows
- **64×64 blocks**, twice as far as before.
- **Color**: every ore in its own color, the ground in its natural colors.
- No flicker of its own (interference can still disturb it).

## Modules
**2 module slots.**

## Upgrade
Smithing table: torch + Iron CRT + [Gold Picture Tube](Picture-Tubes.md) → [Gold CRT](Gold-CRT.md).
