# Netherite CRT

The last upgrade. It hears the living.

> *The heartbeat of what moves in the dark.*

## How to make it
Smithing table: netherite upgrade template + [Diamond CRT](Diamond-CRT.md) + netherite ingot.
No picture tube needed.

## What it shows
Everything the Diamond CRT shows (32/64/128 zoom on **B**, 3 module slots), plus **living things
within about 4 blocks to either side of the cut**, drawn as dots of their size:
- **red** with a heartbeat pulse: hostile,
- **green**: neutral (a wolf, a bee, an enderman, an iron golem). As soon as it attacks, it turns red,
- **yellow**: peaceful (cows, villagers, ...),
- **white stick figures**: other players.

With "Color blind colors" on in the settings, neutral is blue instead of green.

Your game only knows mobs within its entity range, so the far edge of the 128 picture may stay
empty even if something is there.

## Toughness
- **No interference** gets through. Portals and endermen don't disturb it, no lightning rod needed.
- It survives fire and lava, like all netherite.
